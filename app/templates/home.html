Everything a plant owner actually needs

No feeds, no streaks to maintain for their own sake. Just care that happens on time.

Schedules that adapt

Each plant gets watering and feeding intervals drawn from a curated catalog, then scaled to its life stage. Seedlings get watered twice as often as mature plants — automatically.

Reminders before it's too late

A background job sweeps for due plants and sends one digest email — not one per plant. Twenty plants due on the same morning is still a single message.

An AI that knows your garden

Sprout answers gardening questions with your actual plants as context, so "what should I do this week?" gets a real answer instead of a generic one.

Multiple gardens

Balcony, windowsill, terrace, allotment. Keep them separate, switch between them in one click, and move plants across as they grow.

A record of what you did

A growing catalog
